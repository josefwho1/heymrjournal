# Mr Journal quote bank

Checked sources, Nov 2026. In use on the begin screen: Marcus Aurelius + expressive-writing line.

- **Marcus Aurelius**: "The most powerful man in the world kept a private journal. He titled it *To Himself*. We call it *Meditations*." (Meditations, Greek "Ta eis heauton", private notes, AD 161–180; almost certainly never meant for publication.) **In use.**
- **Søren Kierkegaard**: "Life can only be understood backwards; but it must be lived forwards." Shortened from his journal, 1843 (Journals IV A 164). IG post.
- **Joan Didion**: "I write entirely to find out what I'm thinking." Why I Write, 1976. IG post.
- **Expressive writing**: "Fifteen minutes of writing, four days in a row." Pennebaker & Beall, 1986: students who wrote about their deepest thoughts and feelings reported better health four months later, with fewer visits to the health centre. Keep claims modest: it doesn't work for everyone. IG post. **Line in use.**
- **Matthew McConaughey**: "I never wrote things down to remember; I always wrote things down so I could forget." Greenlights, 2020. IG post.
